What this version does

  • Generates a complete Grade 1 lesson pack from subject + topic.
  • Allows each activity to be linked to a strand, sub-strand and specific learning outcome / competency.
  • Provides a separate points/guidance field and material upload area for every activity.
  • Allows teachers to attach material names and local files such as images, PDFs and Office documents to individual activities.
  • Creates learning objectives, activities, materials, assessment questions, marking guide, homework and remedial exercises.
  • Every objective, activity, material, assessment, homework and remedial point has a More details & examples link with teaching tips, worked examples and optional web resources (only the developer can edit these and upload KICD resources, using a PIN).
  • Uploaded files open inside the activity itself (no pop-out): images, PDFs, video, audio, text, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx/.csv) and PowerPoint (.pptx text). WPS files (.wps/.et/.dps) must be saved as Office/PDF first.
  • Saves learner performance locally in the browser.
  • Prints the generated lesson pack.
  • Works offline once the HTML file is saved on the device.

Curriculum note: KICD's Lower Primary materials cover Grades 1–3 and list learning areas including English Activities, Kiswahili, Mathematics, Environmental Activities, Creative Activities and Religious Education. The official KICD designs should remain the authority for exact curriculum alignment.
